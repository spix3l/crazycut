Area and object motion tracking

Track faces, logos, license plates, and objects without complex node graphs. Draw a rectangle around any feature, and CrazyCut solves the motion trajectory frame by frame.

1. Setting up a tracking region

Move the playhead to the frame where your target object is clearly visible. Select the Track Region tool from the preview canvas toolbar and drag a bounding box around the feature.

Click Track Forward or Track Backward. The engine analyzes optical flow and feature points at over 120 fps.

2. Attaching layers and effects

Once a region is solved, you can pin any secondary element to that motion vector:

  • Text & Labels: Attach floating titles or callouts that follow a subject.
  • Blur & Pixelate: Censor faces, sensitive documents, or license plates automatically.
  • Corner Pinning: Replace screens or posters in perspective.

3. Baking into keyframes

If a subject gets occluded or turns away, click Bake to Keyframes. The solved tracking path converts directly into standard bezier keyframe curves on the clip, allowing you to hand-tweak individual frames.
