Auto Shorts and local Whisper AI

Turn long podcast takes and gaming streams into vertical Shorts, TikToks, and Reels. CrazyCut uses bundled whisper.cpp to transcribe audio 100% locally with zero cloud lag.

1. How local transcription works

When you select Auto Shorts on a timeline clip, CrazyCut extracts the audio track and runs it through quantized OpenAI Whisper models running on your local CPU or GPU.

Privacy guarantee

Audio is transcribed directly in memory. No bytes, transcripts, or recordings are sent over the network.

2. Dynamic 9:16 auto-reframing

CrazyCut tracks the speaker or face region and calculates a smooth virtual pan-and-scan path to fit standard 16:9 widescreen rushes into 9:16 vertical canvas format.

  • Speaker Centering: Keeps the active speaker framed automatically.
  • Dwell Threshold: Prevents erratic camera jerks during quick pauses.
  • Keyframe Conversion: Converts automatic camera paths into editable bezier curves anytime.

3. Kinetic subtitle styling

Transcripts are timestamped down to individual words. In the subtitle inspector, you can customize fonts, highlight colors, and animation presets.
